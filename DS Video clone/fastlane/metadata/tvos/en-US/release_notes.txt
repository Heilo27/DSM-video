Profiles: everyone in the house gets their own, and the kids only see what's right for them.

PROFILES
• Up to four profiles per account, each with its own Continue Watching, watchlist and history.
• Give each profile an age rating limit: Under 8, 8–12, 13–16 or 17+. Titles above it are hidden everywhere: Home, libraries, search and Just Added. Unrated titles are hidden on every limited profile.
• Change a profile's age rating and it takes effect at once on every screen. Titles above the new limit can't be played.
• PG films from 1984 or earlier count as 13–16, because PG-13 didn't exist yet.
• Kids' profiles browse by collections like Disney, Pixar and DreamWorks instead of genres. Teen profiles get collections alongside genres, and Adult profiles browse by genre.
• Optional PIN on any profile. Managing profiles always asks for the owner's PIN, and if the owner forgets it, "Forgot PIN" resets it with the DSM password.
• Apple TV asks who's watching each time it opens, since it's shared by the whole room.

NEW
• Cast photos and a More Like This shelf on every title's page.
• Easier first setup: when sign-in or pairing reaches an address that isn't a DSVideo server, Apple TV says so and shows the address and a QR code for installing the server. A short welcome shows how many libraries and videos were found.

PLAYBACK
• If your network drops mid-movie, playback now reconnects right away and picks up where you were. If it can't within 20 seconds, it shows the problem with a Retry button.
• Streaming over QuickConnect now uses the address you connected through.

SHOW PAGES
• A Resume or Play button right on the show page.
• Cleaner focus: no more white boxes behind buttons, sort chips and season rows.
• The show page layout no longer pushes the title off screen, and library screens show a clear heading with the item count.

PAIRING
• Sign in your iPhone or iPad with a code: choose Pair iOS Device at the top of Home, then enter the 6-digit code on the device.

SERVER UPDATES
• If your DSVideo server is out of date, the app tells you once and suggests updating it.

TOP SHELF
• On a family account, the Top Shelf shows only titles safe for the youngest profile, and never anyone's watch history.

FIXES
• Clearer messages when your NAS can't be reached: the app now names the real cause and the next step instead of a vague error.
• Connection fixes for QuickConnect and host-name sign-in.
• Remote buttons no longer control a hidden player behind an error screen.
• Watching on two devices: an older position saved offline no longer overwrites a newer one, or un-watches a title you finished.
• Catching up: a device that was missing a few titles or showing out-of-date ratings now fixes itself with a one-time refresh.
• You can create a second profile when your account only has one.
• PIN errors, such as codes that don't match or a PIN that couldn't be saved, now stay on screen instead of disappearing.

Profiles need DSVideo Server 0.3.9 or later on your NAS. The newest fixes (old PG films, sync catch-up) need server 0.3.9-0005. Cast photos, More Like This and QuickConnect streaming need the latest server update (free download).
