This update fixes Apple TV pairing, player controls, and keeping your place.

PAIRING AND SIGN-IN
• A first-time Apple TV can now pair. The flow previously could not complete at all.
• Home screen rails now load right after pairing instead of staying empty.
• Non-admin accounts can now sign in — a bad session parameter was rejecting them.
• Unreachable servers no longer report as "wrong password."

PLAYER CONTROLS
• Playback speed and subtitle buttons are now reachable with the remote.
• "Mark Unwatched" now works reliably.
• Fixed playback progress being lost or reverting to an older position.

FIND IT FASTER
• New multi-select genre filter — combine genres to narrow your library.
• Your Watchlist now loads. It previously came up empty on every device.

Plus more dependable artwork loading, better show matching, and general performance refinements.

Thanks for using DSM Video on Apple TV. Keep the feedback coming.
