Just say what you want to watch, and your Home screen finally stays up to date.

SIRI
• Hold the microphone button and say "Play Blade Runner in DSM Video" or "Find Blade Runner in DSM Video" — it opens straight to the title. No more typing on the on-screen keyboard.

A HOME SCREEN THAT'S CURRENT
• Just Added now comes from your server rather than being guessed at on the device, so recently added films actually appear. A show that gained a season counts as one new thing instead of twelve, so it can't push everything else off the row.
• New Suggested row, picked by genre from what you've been watching.

SHOWS THAT WERE HIDING
• A series split across two folders, or with only some episodes matched, now appears once instead of twice — or not at all. Star Trek: The Next Generation and others are back.
• Episode names are correct where they used to show the filename.

REMOTE AND FOCUS
• Play is selected when you open a video, instead of Next Episode.
• Next Episode is no longer oversized next to Play.

PLAYBACK
• HEVC videos that played as audio-only now play properly.
• Scrubbing repeatedly no longer locks you out of your own video.
• Faster startup, smoother seeking, and deeper buffering.

CONNECTION
• The app switches between your home network and remote access on its own, without a restart.
• The library keeps syncing instead of quietly stopping while appearing fine.
