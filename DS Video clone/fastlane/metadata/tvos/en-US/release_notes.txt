This update fixes the remote controls in the player, adds mark as watched, and brings your Watchlist to Apple TV.

PLAYER CONTROLS THAT WORK
• Every button in the player is now reachable with the remote — skip to start, back 15, forward 15, and skip to end previously could not be selected at all.
• Playback speed is now also in Settings. If your videos were stuck playing fast with no way to change it, this fixes it.
• Menu now closes the controls first instead of exiting playback entirely.
• Pressing Menu during the next-episode countdown no longer starts the next episode anyway.

NEW ON APPLE TV
• Your Watchlist now appears on the Home screen. Saved items were previously impossible to view.
• Mark as Watched and Mark as Unwatched on any video.

FIXES
• Long descriptions no longer push the cast and Next Episode button off screen where the remote couldn't reach them.
• Content sits properly inside the safe area, so nothing crops at the edges of your TV.
• Signing in no longer shows an old error message while a correct password is working.
• If your NAS drops off the network mid-video, you get a clear message instead of an endless spinner.

Plus consistent empty screens and layout polish throughout.

Thanks for using DSM Video on Apple TV. Keep the feedback coming.
