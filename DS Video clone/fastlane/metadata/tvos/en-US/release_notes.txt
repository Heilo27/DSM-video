A big quality-of-life update for the Apple TV experience.

RESUME WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
Open any TV show and the app automatically lands on your last-watched season and episode — so the next thing to watch is always right there waiting.

ARE YOU STILL WATCHING?
After four consecutive auto-played episodes, the app pauses and asks if you're still watching. A 20-second countdown gives you time to confirm. If you walk away, playback stops and you're taken back to the show page.

WATCHLIST ON APPLE TV
Bookmark movies and TV episodes directly from Apple TV. The watchlist button now appears alongside Play and Start Over.

SORT OPTIONS
The TV Shows grid now exposes all four sort options from the toolbar: Recently Watched, A→Z, Recently Added, and Release Year.

NEXT EPISODE BUTTON
The Next Episode button now appears on the episode detail screen, giving you a shortcut to the next episode without going back to the season list.

PLAYER IMPROVEMENTS
• Skip to start and skip to end buttons added to the transport strip
• Play/pause button has a subtle background ring for better visibility
• D-pad seek moves in 10-second steps (was 30)
• Playback speed is preserved when resuming after pause

BUG FIXES
• Season list auto-expands to the correct resume season after loading
• Fixed a case where multiple seasons could expand simultaneously
• Bonjour server discovery updated to use the modern NWBrowser API
• Pairing code screen now hidden in the App Switcher
