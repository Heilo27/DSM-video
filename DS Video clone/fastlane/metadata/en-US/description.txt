DSM Video brings your personal Synology NAS video library to Apple TV. Browse and stream your movies and TV shows from the comfort of your living room — no subscriptions, no cloud storage, no compromise on privacy.

YOUR LIBRARY, ON THE BIG SCREEN
Stream movies and TV shows stored on your Synology NAS directly to Apple TV. DSM Video scans your library automatically and organises your content with rich metadata — posters, episode guides, and descriptions.

SEAMLESS iOS PAIRING
Already using DSM Video on your iPhone or iPad? Pair your Apple TV in seconds using a simple on-screen code. Your account and server connections carry over instantly — no re-entering passwords or server addresses.

CONTINUE WATCHING
Pick up right where you left off. Continue Watching keeps track of your progress across every device, so you can start a film on your phone and finish it in the living room without missing a beat.

JUST ADDED
See what's new in your library the moment it arrives. The Just Added rail surfaces recently added movies and episodes so you always know what's fresh.

SMART DISCOVERY
Your NAS is found automatically on your home network using Bonjour. No manual IP addresses required. For remote access, DSM Video supports Synology QuickConnect and HTTPS connections.

SEARCH
Find any title in your library instantly. Search across movies and TV shows with a fast, keyboard-friendly interface optimised for Apple TV.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
DSM Video connects directly to your Synology NAS. Your media never passes through third-party servers. Everything stays in your home.

Compatible with Synology DiskStation NAS running DSM 6 or later. Requires a compatible DSM video library.
