This update focuses on getting you signed in, keeping your place, and finding what you want to watch.

SIGNING IN THAT ACTUALLY WORKS
• Non-admin accounts can now sign in — a bad session parameter was rejecting them outright.
• Unreachable servers no longer report as "wrong password," so you can tell a network problem from a login problem.
• Connection errors now say what actually went wrong instead of failing silently.
• Signing out properly ends your session on the server.

FIND IT FASTER
• New multi-select genre filter — combine genres to narrow your library.
• Your Watchlist now loads. It previously came up empty on every device.
• Fixed library grids on iPad not reloading when you switch libraries in the sidebar.

KEEPING YOUR PLACE
• "Mark Unwatched" now works reliably.
• Fixed playback progress being lost or reverting to an older position.
• Moving a file no longer signs you out.

Plus improved artwork loading, better filename and show matching, and a round of interface polish.

Thanks for using DSM Video. Keep the feedback coming.
