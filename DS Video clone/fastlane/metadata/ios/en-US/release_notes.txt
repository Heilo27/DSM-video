Profiles: everyone in the house gets their own, and the kids only see what's right for them.

PROFILES
• Up to four profiles per account, each with its own Continue Watching, watchlist and history.
• Give each profile an age rating limit. Titles above it are hidden everywhere: Home, libraries, search, Just Added and downloads. Unrated titles are hidden on every limited profile.
• Kids' profiles browse by collections like Disney, Pixar and DreamWorks instead of genres. Teen profiles get collections alongside genres, and Adult profiles browse by genre.
• Optional PIN on any profile. Managing profiles always asks for the owner's PIN, and if the owner forgets it, "Forgot PIN" resets it with the DSM password.
• Your iPhone remembers which profile you picked.

APPLE TV
• Sign in without typing your password: on a signed-in Apple TV choose Pair iOS Device, then enter the 6-digit code it shows on your iPhone.

SERVER UPDATES
• If your DSVideo server is out of date, the app tells you once and suggests updating it.

DOWNLOADS
• Downloads follow the profile's age limit, even offline, and each profile resumes from its own spot.
• A download that finishes while the app is closed is kept instead of discarded, and tapping Download twice no longer starts a second copy.

FIXES
• Clearer messages when your NAS can't be reached: the app now names the real cause and the next step instead of a vague error.
• Connection fixes: signing in with a host name such as localhost, and QuickConnect failures, now work or explain why.
• If the offline library has to be rebuilt, the app tells you.
• Watching on two devices: an older position saved offline no longer overwrites a newer one, or un-watches a title you finished.
• You can create a second profile when your account only has one.

Profiles need DSVideo Server 0.3.9 or later on your NAS (free download).
