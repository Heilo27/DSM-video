This update fixes playback interruptions, adds mark as watched, and makes search and your watchlist reachable everywhere.

PLAYBACK THAT RECOVERS
• A phone call, Siri, or another app no longer leaves playback stuck — audio resumes properly afterward.
• Unplugging headphones or removing AirPods now pauses cleanly instead of leaving the player in a stuck state.
• If your NAS drops off the network mid-video, you now get a clear message and can retry, instead of an endless loading spinner.
• Audio keeps playing when you lock your screen. You can turn this off in Settings.

NEW CONTROLS
• Mark as Watched and Mark as Unwatched — clear a stray resume point or catch up on something you watched elsewhere.
• Playback Speed is now in Settings, so it's easy to find and easy to set back to normal.

FIND YOUR STUFF
• Search now works from the Home screen on iPhone and searches your whole library, not just one section.
• Your Watchlist is now reachable on iPad.
• Play is clearly unavailable for videos that aren't downloaded when your NAS is offline, instead of failing after you tap.

ACCESSIBILITY
• The launch animation is skipped when Reduce Motion is on.
• Better VoiceOver labels on setup and diagnostics, and text that scales properly at larger sizes.

Plus consistent empty screens and a round of layout polish.

Thanks for using DSM Video. Keep the feedback coming.
