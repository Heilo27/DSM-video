This update focuses on a smoother, more reliable library and playback experience.

• Fixed TV show posters that could appear blank or shift position while scrolling, and posters that occasionally overflowed onto neighboring shows.
• Shows that share a folder (such as a series and its spin-off) now appear as separate, correctly-rendered entries.
• Improved playback reliability for MKV and AVI titles when paired with the latest server update — fewer "playback failed" interruptions.
• More dependable artwork loading throughout the app, including after reconnecting.
