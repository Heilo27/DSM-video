Ask Siri for a movie, and your Home screen finally stays up to date.

SIRI
• "Play Blade Runner in DSM Video" or "Find Blade Runner in DSM Video" opens it straight to the title — no typing on a remote.

HOME SCREEN THAT'S ACTUALLY CURRENT
• Just Added now comes from your server instead of being guessed at on the device, so films you added an hour ago show up. A show that gained a whole season counts as one new thing, not twelve, so it can't bury everything else.
• New Suggested row, picked by genre from what you've been watching.
• Rows no longer go stale after a pull-to-refresh.

SHOWS THAT WERE HIDING
• A series split across two folders, or with only some episodes matched, now appears once instead of twice — or not at all. Star Trek: The Next Generation and others are back.
• Episode names are correct where they used to show the filename.

HOME AND AWAY
• The app now switches between your home network and remote access on its own. Come home and it notices, without a restart or a trip to Settings.
• The library keeps syncing instead of quietly stopping and looking fine.

PLAYBACK
• HEVC videos that played as audio-only now play properly.
• Scrubbing repeatedly no longer locks you out of your own video.
• Faster startup and smoother seeking, with deeper buffering.
• Downloads that failed silently now actually download.

FIXES
• Only one volume indicator on screen.
• Bigger, more reliable tap targets, and buttons that no longer look available when they aren't.
• Error messages name the real problem instead of guessing.
