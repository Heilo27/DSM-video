DSM Video turns your iPhone or iPad into a window to your Synology NAS video library. Browse and stream your personal collection of movies and TV shows from anywhere — at home or over the internet — with no subscriptions, no cloud storage, and no compromise on privacy.

YOUR LIBRARY, IN YOUR POCKET
Stream movies and TV shows stored on your Synology NAS directly to your iPhone or iPad. DSM Video scans your library automatically and organises your content with rich metadata — posters, episode guides, and descriptions pulled straight from your server.

PAIR WITH APPLE TV
Already using DSM Video on Apple TV? Pair your iPhone or iPad in seconds using a simple on-screen code. Your account and server connections carry over instantly — no re-entering passwords or server addresses.

CONTINUE WATCHING
Pick up right where you left off. Continue Watching keeps track of your progress across every device, so you can start a film on your TV and finish it on your phone without missing a beat.

JUST ADDED
See what's new in your library the moment it arrives. The Just Added rail surfaces recently added movies and episodes so you always know what's fresh.

SMART DISCOVERY
Your NAS is found automatically on your home network using Bonjour. No manual IP addresses required. For remote access, DSM Video supports Synology QuickConnect and direct HTTPS connections — stream your library from anywhere with an internet connection.

SEARCH
Find any title in your library instantly. Search across movies and TV shows with a fast, intuitive interface.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
DSM Video connects directly to your Synology NAS. Your media never passes through third-party servers. Everything stays in your home.

Compatible with Synology DiskStation NAS running DSM 6 or later. Requires a compatible DSM video library.
